CONCLUSION

      We have tried to present                   here with adequate m o t i v a t i o n         a theory of
constructions        and to show how it is in harmony                         with BROUWER'S        program -
at least as the p r o g r a m has been e x p l a i n e d by HEYTING.                      We consider the
attempt     rather successful,              but much remains              to be done.      For example,        we
have not d i s c u s s e d    quantification              over species        (better     : subspecies        of

After further discussions                    with KREISEL and G~DEL it has become                               clear
that the attempt to eliminate                       "proofs"            (as abstract          objects)        and to
