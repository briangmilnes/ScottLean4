# The Publications of Dana S. Scott — A Bibliography

Dana Stewart Scott (b. 1932) is the Hillman University Professor Emeritus of Computer Science, Philosophy, and Mathematical Logic at Carnegie Mellon University. He shared the 1976 ACM Turing Award with Michael O. Rabin for their joint paper on nondeterministic finite automata, and is co-creator (with Christopher Strachey) of denotational semantics and the founder of domain theory.

This bibliography is compiled from his [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/pid/s/DanaSScott.html), his [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=oaja5KYAAAAJ), and the community-maintained [*Selected Papers of Dana S. Scott*](https://github.com/CMU-HoTT/scott) repository (CMU-HoTT), which hosts author-scanned open-access PDFs. Only works verifiable through these sources are listed. Where an open-access PDF was confirmed to resolve, a direct link is given; otherwise the entry is marked *paywalled / no OA PDF found*.
