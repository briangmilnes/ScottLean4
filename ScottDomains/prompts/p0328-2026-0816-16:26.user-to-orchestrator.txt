## Regular co-well-poweredness

Not a corollary of the well-powered case, as I flagged. The dual runs in `Equᵒᵖ`, where the one-point space that detected points has no counterpart. What replaces it is the manuscript's other half — *"forming a regular quotient is just making the equivalence relation coarser"* — made precise: a regular quotient is determined by its **kernel relation** `{(x,y) | q x ≡ q y}`, living in `Set (|𝒜| × |𝒜|)`, again `Type u`.
