## `completeLattice_boundedComplete`

One line:

```lean
instance completeLattice_boundedComplete {L : Type u} [CompleteLattice L] :
    ScottDomains.BoundedComplete L where
  isLUB_sSup_of_bddAbove s _ := isLUB_sSup s
```

The `BddAbove` hypothesis is discarded — `sSup s` bounds every subset, bounded or not. It's sound without a compatibility side condition because `CompleteLattice.toCompletePartialOrder` is defined with `sSup := sSup`, so the `SupSet` the `CompletePartialOrder` carries is literally the lattice's own.

## `ALat`

`AlgebraicLattice` (complete + `IsAlgebraic`) with `ScottHom` morphisms, as a `LargeCategory`. Unlike `Equ` and `PEqu`, morphisms here are honest functions rather than equivalence classes — nothing to quotient by — so the laws are `ext`-then-`rfl`.

## The payoff

```lean
theorem isAlgebraic_scottHom (A B : AlgebraicLattice.{u}) :
    ScottDomains.IsAlgebraic (ScottHom A.carrier B.carrier) :=
```
