**§5.5 is finished.** `dInfinity : DInfinityFoundations (TokenIdeal dInfTokens)` — pushed as `36ecba3`. Build: **846 jobs, zero errors, zero warnings, zero `sorry`.**

**How the obstruction fell.** A token is a pair `(n, x) : Nat × DTok` — a *declared level* and a flat token — compared after lifting both to the max of the declared levels. `cmpTok_up`/`cmpTok_eq` show any admissible comparison level gives the same answer; that is `leAt_embTok` plus arithmetic.

The step that made it work: **nothing forces `x` to be well formed at level `n`, and nothing needs to.** A token declared too low is read through `isTopTok` and is therefore order-equivalent to ⊥ — and extra copies of ⊥ change no ideal. That is what lets the carrier be a plain product instead of a subtype, and it is exactly why the whole construction avoids dependent types and transports, which is what stopped the indexed tower.

Joins are total (inherited from D₀ = 𝕊 being a lattice) and are concatenation of step-sets; `joinLift` carries that through lifting by induction on the number of lifts, with the comparison level dropping alongside. Countability is `dtoksUpTo`, proved covering by a **mutual** recursion over `DTok` and its list.

**One measurement worth your attention.** `dInfinity` consumes `[lem, propext, Quot.sound]` — exactly what every other witness consumes, and **no `Classical.choice`**. It nearly did: an earlier `dLe_lub` closed an impossible branch with a bare `omega` whose *goal was not arithmetic*, and omega discharges such goals through `Classical.byContradiction`. That would have been the development's first use of choice outside the ℝ module. `absurd hN (by omega)` — omega proving the arithmetic fact, `absurd` doing the eliminating — removes it. Worth knowing generally: `omega` is choice-free on arithmetic goals, not on arbitrary ones.
