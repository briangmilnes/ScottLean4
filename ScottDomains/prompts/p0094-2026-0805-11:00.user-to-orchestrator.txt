Grounded in what's actually on disk (v4.32.2): **`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/` is ~1,089 files** — one of the largest formalized category-theory libraries anywhere. Here is the shape of it.

## Foundations
- **`Category`** (the class): objects in `Type u`, hom-types `X ⟶ Y` in `Type v` (universe-polymorphic, so small vs. large is handled by universes), identity `𝟙 X`, composition written **diagrammatically** `f ≫ g` ("`f` then `g`"), and the three laws. A `Category` extends `Quiver` (a graph) — quivers are the substrate.
- **`Functor`** (`C ⥤ D`), **`NatTrans`** / **`NatIso`**, **`Iso`** (isomorphisms), **`Equivalence`** of categories, and **Yoneda** (the embedding and the lemma).

## Universal constructions — the bulk
- **`Limits`** — *208 files*: cones/cocones, all (co)limits, products/coproducts, equalizers, pullbacks/pushouts, terminal/initial objects, **Kan extensions**, filtered colimits, and the whole preservation/reflection/creation apparatus.
- **`Adjunction`** (22): units/counits, reflective/coreflective subcategories, adjoint-functor theorems, monadicity.
- **`Comma`** (17), `ComposableArrows`, over/under (slice) categories, `WithTerminal`.

## Monoidal, enriched, higher
- **`Monoidal`** — *108 files*: monoidal, braided, symmetric, **closed** and **cartesian-closed**, rigid/autonomous categories, coherence, monoidal functors (this is where Mathlib's CCC lives, unlike the hand-rolled one in your `Playground/CartesianClosed.lean`).
- **`Enriched`** (10), **`Bicategory`** (44: 2-categories, pseudofunctors), `Center`.

## The homological tower
- **`Preadditive`** (34) → `Linear` → **`Abelian`** (56: kernels/cokernels, exactness) → **`Triangulated`** (30) with **`Shift`** (17) → **`Localization`** (40: calculus of fractions, the machinery behind derived categories). Also `Idempotents` (Karoubi envelope), `GradedObject`, `Subobject`.

## Topos-theoretic / geometric
- **`Sites`** — *113 files*: Grothendieck topologies, sheaves on a site, sheafification.
- **`Topos`** (elementary topoi), **`Galois`** (11: Galois categories & the fundamental group), `LocallyCartesianClosed`, `Distributive`, `EffectiveEpi`, `RegularCategory`, `Adhesive`.

## Monads & special categories
- **`Monad`** (12): monads, **Eilenberg–Moore** and **Kleisli** categories, `Endofunctor`.
- **`Groupoid`, `Discrete`, `FinCategory`, `Filtered`, `Types`** (the category of Lean types), **`ConcreteCategory`** (a faithful functor to `Type` — forgetful functors), `Quotient`, `PathCategory` (free category on a quiver).

## The everyday categories (living elsewhere)
Concrete categories of structures are filed under their subject, not `CategoryTheory/`: **`Algebra/Category/`** (`MonCat`, `GrpCat`, `RingCat`, `ModuleCat`, …), **`Order/Category/`** (`Lat`, `DistLat`, `HeytAlg`, `Preord`, `PartOrd`, …), **`Topology/Category/`** (`TopCat`, `Profinite`, …) — each with (co)completeness and forgetful/free adjunctions proved.

## Two design points worth knowing
- **Automation:** there's a dedicated tactic **`aesop_cat`** that discharges the naturality/coherence side-goals, so most of the bookkeeping is automated; `simp` carries the rewriting.
- **Modern frontier:** `Presentable`/accessible categories (18), the **small-object argument** (`SmallObject`), `MorphismProperty`/`ObjectProperty` (classes of maps/objects — the model-category-flavored infrastructure), `LiftingProperties`.
