## Two reading cautions

**A `Prop`-valued `def` is a statement, not a theorem.** `Theorem310NotWellPowered` — footnote 4's "`Equ` is not well-powered" — is the one such left. It carries no `sorry`, so a zero count doesn't tell you it's unproved. That's why the README leads with the distinction, and why I got it wrong myself at r0087.

**The module docstrings carry the mathematics.** Each quotes the paper passage it formalizes and says where the encoding departs, and why — e.g. 3.13 is stated with `· × B` rather than Mathlib's `prod.functor`, which is `B ⨯ ·` and routes through `Classical.choice`. If you read only the statements you'll miss those.
