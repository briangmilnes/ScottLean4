## Limit 1 is gone — and it was my error, not a restriction of the mathematics

`funTokens` and `funSpace` now take **arbitrary token posets on both sides**. Before changing anything I grepped for `Q.join` in the construction: it never appeared. The `JoinTokens` hypothesis was decoration.

My earlier justification was wrong on its own terms. I claimed only *consistent* step sets are legitimate tokens, that consistency is undecidable, and that the carrier would therefore need a subtype with an undecidable predicate, blocking `enum` without choice. **`stepEntails_of_unbounded`** (axiom-free) shows why that is false: a step set whose contributions at `a` have no upper bound entails **everything** at `a` — the premise "v bounds the contributions" is unsatisfiable, so the implication is vacuous. An inconsistent token sits *high in the order* rather than being ill-formed, so the carrier can be all finite lists.

What total joins actually buy is the **reading** of the result: with them no token is inconsistent and the ideals are [D → E]; without them the inconsistent tokens sit above everything and the construction builds [D → E] with a top adjoined. §4's dualizing object [D → 𝕊] is the former case.

## Limit 2 is half closed

**`funApply`** proves the operative content: each ideal of step functions *acts* on ideals of arguments, producing an ideal, with `funApply_mono_left` / `funApply_mono_right` giving monotonicity in both arguments.
