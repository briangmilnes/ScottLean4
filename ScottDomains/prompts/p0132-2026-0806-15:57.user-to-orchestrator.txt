## r0021 — Lemma 8 complete (`6db6e8e`)

Seventh of the 29. The two directions of currying are not symmetric:

**Currying is easy.** Fixing `x`, the map `y ↦ (x, y)` sends a directed set to one whose first-coordinate image is the singleton `{x}` and whose second is `s`, so `isLUB_prod` splits the goal into `isLUB_singleton` and the hypothesis.

**Uncurrying is the work.** The goal is `g (⨆ fst s) (⨆ snd s) ⊑ u` where the two suprema run over *different projections of the same directed set*. Peeling them one at a time — continuity of `g`, then of `g x` — leaves a goal about `g x y` with `x` and `y` drawn from **different members** of `s`. Directedness of `s` in the *product* is what supplies a single member above both, and that step has no analogue in the easy direction.
