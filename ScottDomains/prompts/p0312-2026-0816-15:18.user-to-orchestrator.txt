## What stopped it

The product projections in `PEqu` need `Topology.IsScott (A.prod B).carrier Set.univ`. Instance resolution won't find it — even though `PartialEquilogicalSpace.isScott` *is* an instance and `(A.prod B).isScott` is exactly that fact.

**It's an instance clash, not a missing lemma.** `(A.prod B).carrier` is definitionally `A.carrier × B.carrier`, so Mathlib's `instTopologicalSpaceProd` competes with the object's own `topologicalSpace` field. When the product topology wins, `IsScott` of that carrier becomes a claim about the **product** topology — a different, generally strictly coarser topology than the Scott topology of the product lattice. No instance exists for it, and none should: the statement is false.

This is the mirror of what I told you last turn. I said nothing needed proving about Scott topologies and products because the field asks for the Scott topology *of the product lattice*.
