## 1. The axioms, read from the PDF

`pdftotext -layout papers/Gunter Scott 1990.pdf`, page 28. Quoted verbatim, with
the operation glyph restored (see the caveat below) and the paper's `]`/`[`/`\`
rendering of `♯`/`♭`/`♮` restored:

> **Definition:** A continuous algebra (of signature `(2)`) is a cpo `E` together
> with a continuous binary function `⋓ : E × E → E`. We refer to the following
> collection of axioms on `⋓` as theory `T♮`:
>
> 1. associativity: `(r ⋓ s) ⋓ t = r ⋓ (s ⋓ t)`
> 2. commutativity: `r ⋓ s = s ⋓ r`
> 3. idempotence: `s ⋓ s = s`.
>
> (These are the well-known semi-lattice axioms.) A homomorphism between
> continuous algebras `D` and `E` is a continuous function `f : D → E` such that
> `f (s ⋓ t) = f (s) ⋓ f (t)` for all `s, t ∈ D`.

> Now, consider the following axiom:
>
> `4♯. s ⋓ t ⊑ s.`
>
> Let `T♯` be the set of axioms obtained by adding axiom `4♯` to the axioms in
> `T♮`. Similarly, let `T♭` be obtained by adding the axiom
>
> `4♭. s ⊑ s ⋓ t`
>
> to the axioms in `T♮`.

And the theorem itself:

> **Theorem 12** Let `D` be a domain. Suppose `⟨E, ⋓⟩` is a continuous algebra
> which satisfies `T♮`. For any continuous `f : D → E`, there is a unique
> homomorphism `ext(f) : D♮ → E` which completes the following diagram: […]
>
> **Proof:** (Hint) If `u = {x₁, …, xₙ} ∈ s ∈ D♮`, and `û` is the principal
> ideal generated by `u`, then define `ext(f)(û) = f(x₁) ⋓ ⋯ ⋓ f(xₙ)`. This
> function has a unique continuous extension to all of `D♮` given by
> `ext(f)(s) = ⨆{ext(f)(û) | u ∈ s}`.

> The point is this: Theorem 12 still holds when `D♮` and `T♮` are replaced by
> `D♯` and `T♯` respectively, or by `D♭` and `T♭` respectively.

### Does this match r0031's report as relayed in the plan?

**Yes, on the axioms; the plan is wrong on one name.** The plan quotes r0031 as

> a continuous algebra `⊕ : E × E → E` with associativity, commutativity and
> idempotence; `T♯` adds `s ⊕ t ⊑ s`, and `T♭` adds `s ⊑ s ⊕ t`.

Every clause of that is confirmed verbatim, including the orientation of both
strengthenings — this is the place a paraphrase would most easily invert, and it
is not inverted.

The one correction: the base theory is **`T♮`**, not an undecorated `T`. The
paper decorates all three theories and all three powerdomains with musical
accidentals — `♮` convex (Plotkin), `♯` upper (Smyth), `♭` lower (Hoare) — and
`T♮` is the theory whose free algebra is `D♮`. `docs/PaperInventory.md` row 4.5
and the r0032 plan both write `T`. `pdftotext` renders `♮` as `\`, `♯` as `]` and
`♭` as `[`, which is where the accidental was lost.

### The operation glyph

The paper's own glyph for the algebra operation **does not survive text
extraction** — `pdftotext` drops it, with and without `-layout`, in all six
places it occurs on page 28 (`function  : E  E ! E`, `(r  s)  t = r  (s  t)`,
…). I could not recover it. In §5.3, where the operation is exhibited on a
powerdomain, the paper writes a decorated `∪`.

The Lean file writes `⋓` and not the `⊕` of r0031's report and the plan, for a
mechanical reason: `⊕` is Lean core's `infixr:30` notation for `Sum`, and
declaring a second parse of it makes every axiom of `T♮` an overload-resolution
failure — that was the first build's first six errors. `⋓` is free in Mathlib and
is closer to the paper's decorated `∪` anyway. The module docstring records this.

## 2. What "initial" means here, exactly

Stated as `∃!` over the function, with the predicate carrying *both* clauses, so
neither existence nor uniqueness can be silently dropped:

```lean
theorem thm12 (hf : ScottContinuous f) (hmono : Monotone (foldGen (A := A) f)) :
    ∃! h : IdealCompletion A → E, IsHom h ∧ ∀ x : D, h (unit x) = f x
```

where

* `IsHom h` is the paper's own sentence — `structure IsHom` with fields
  `scottContinuous : ScottContinuous h` and `map_op : ∀ s t, h (s ⋓ t) = h s ⋓ h t`;
* `unit : D → IdealCompletion A` is the paper's `{|·|}`, the left leg of the
  diagram, and `∀ x, h (unit x) = f x` is "completes the diagram";
* `∃!` is Lean's — existence of `ext f` **and** uniqueness against every rival.

Uniqueness is through `{|·|}`, not through the principal ideals. That is strictly
stronger, and closing the gap is `principal_eq_fold_unit`:
`↓u = {|x₁|} ⋓ ⋯ ⋓ {|xₙ|}`, i.e. `D` generates the powerdomain.

`[IsAlgebraic D]` is the whole hypothesis on `D`. Countability of `K(D)` — the
last clause of *domain* — is **not** used by Theorem 12; it is what makes `Dᴾ` a
domain (Theorem 11), which is a separate statement.

## 4. Structure of the proof, and where the theory is spent

Eight sections, of which 1–6 are generic in the carrier and 7 instantiates.

1. **`Binop E`** — the paper's *continuous algebra of signature `(2)`*, with
   **joint** Scott continuity of `E × E → E` on the product cpo of
   `Product.lean`, which is the paper's literal reading. `IsSemilattice` /
   `IsUpper` / `IsLower` are `T♮` / `T♯` / `T♭`, three classes, not one flag.
   Derived: under `T♭`, `⋓` is the binary join for `⊑`; under `T♯`, the binary
   meet; under `T♮`, neither.

2. **The fold `f(x₁) ⋓ ⋯ ⋓ f(xₙ)`.** Enumeration-independence is not re-proved:
   axioms 1–3 make `⋓` a semilattice operation, so `AlgOrder E` carries the
   *algebraic* order `a ≼ b ↔ a ⋓ b = b` as a `SemilatticeSup`, and `fold` is
   Mathlib's `Finset.sup'` there. This buys `sup'_singleton`, `sup'_union`,
   `sup'_image`, `sup'_cons`. `instSemilatticeSupAlgOrder` is the one declaration
   in the file that depends on **no axioms at all**.

3. **Monotonicity of the fold**, once per ordering. `fold_le_fold_of_hoare` and
   `fold_le_fold_of_smyth` are four lines each, because under `T♭`/`T♯` the fold
   is a join/meet. The convex case `fold_le_fold_of_convex` is where `T♮` costs
   real work: with `φ` a Hoare witness and `ψ` a Smyth witness,

       fold u f = fold u f ⋓ fold v (f ∘ ψ)     (ψ '' v ⊆ u, so the union is u)
                ⊑ fold u (f ∘ φ) ⋓ fold v f     (f a ⊑ f (φ a), f (ψ b) ⊑ f b)
                = fold v f                      (φ '' u ⊆ v, so the union is v)

   Idempotence is spent in the two union collapses; associativity and
   commutativity inside `fold_union`; monotonicity of `⋓` in the middle step.

4. **The universal property of the ideal completion.** The paper's *"This
   function has a unique continuous extension to all of `D♮`"* mentions neither
   `⋓` nor the axioms, so it is proved in that generality: for any pre-order `A`
   with a least element, any cpo `E` and any monotone `g : A → E`,
   `idealExtend g I = ⨆ (g '' I)` is the unique Scott-continuous map with
   `idealExtend g ↓a = g a`. Reusable beyond Theorem 12.

5. **`⋓` on a powerdomain.** `s ⋓ t = {w | ∃ u ∈ s, ∃ v ∈ t, w ⊑ u ∪ v}` — the
   paper's definition — shown to be an ideal, **jointly continuous**, and to
   satisfy `T♮`. Given once over a `FinSets` presentation rather than three
   times.

6. **Theorem 12** — `unit` (`{|·|}`, proved continuous, with compactness of the
   generators spent in leastness), `ext f`, `isHom_ext`, `ext_unit`,
   `principal_eq_fold_unit`, and `thm12`.

7. **The three instances.** `FinSets ↥(compacts D) A` for
   `Hoare.Pf ↥(compacts D)` (a `Finset` subtype), `Smyth.Basis D` (a `structure`)
   and `Plotkin.FinCompacts D` (a `Set` subtype, so `toFinset` goes through
   `Set.Finite.toFinset`). Each `thm12_*` is then four to eight lines.

8. **Witnesses.** `example : IsSemilattice (Hoare/Smyth/Plotkin.Powerdomain Prop)`,
   `IsUpper (Smyth.Powerdomain Prop)`, `IsLower (Hoare.Powerdomain Prop)` — an
   instance never demanded is never checked.
