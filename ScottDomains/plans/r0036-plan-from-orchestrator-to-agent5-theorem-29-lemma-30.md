## The goal

## The route, and why it is cheaper than it looks

The key structural observation: `Plus D = IdealCompletion (MPair ↥(compacts D))`,
so **the iteration happens at the level of countable posets, not domains**. Build
the chain of bases, take its colimit as a poset, and apply Theorem 11 once at the
end:

| # | Step | Ingredient on `main` |
| -- | ---- | -------------------- |
| 1 | `A₀ = PUnit`, `Aₙ₊₁ = MPair Aₙ` — a chain of countable posets | `MPair`, `BifiniteUniversal.lean:144` |
| 2 | `eta : Aₙ → Aₙ₊₁` is an order embedding | `eta` (196), `eta_le_eta_iff` (211) — proved |
| 3 | Each `Aₙ` is countable and a Plotkin order | `isPlotkinOrder_MPair` (372), `MSub_finite` (308), `MSub_isNormalIn` (337) |
| 4 | `A∞` = the ω-colimit of the chain along `eta` | **to build** |
| 5 | `A∞` countable, and a Plotkin order | to prove, from 3 by a colimit argument |
| 6 | `V = IdealCompletion A∞`, hence `Domain V` | `IdealCompletion.thm11`, r0028 — the ideal completion of a countable pre-order is a domain |
| 7 | `V ≅ V⁺` | to prove — the colimit is the fixed point |

Step 4 is the one real construction and it is where the r0035 restart plan warned
the cost lies: **dependent transport across `Aₙ → Aₙ₊ₖ`**. Two shapes are
available and the choice matters:

1. `Σ n, Aₙ` quotiented by "equal after enough `eta`s" — the standard direct
   limit. Transport is by `Nat.rec` on the difference of indices, and every proof
   about `A∞` becomes a `Quot.ind` plus a transport lemma.
2. A `Quot` over the sigma type with the relation generated by `⟨n, x⟩ ~ ⟨n+1, eta x⟩`,
   which makes the generating step definitional and pushes the induction into the
   relation.

Prototype both on the *order* alone before proving anything about Plotkin
structure. The cost of this stream is dominated by which shape makes
`decide`-free transport lemmas shortest, not by the mathematics.

**Theorem 11 is doing the heavy lifting** — it means `V` needs no cpo
construction, no directed-sup definition, and no bounded-completeness reasoning.
Get `A∞` right as a countable poset and `V`'s domain structure is `inferInstance`.

## Two warnings from r0034, both kernel-checked

`BifiniteUniversal.lean` records two defects in §7.4 as printed, both confirmed
in Lean; the module already works around them and you inherit the workaround:

1. The printed relation is **not reflexive** — `(⊥, ∅)` fails `b ⊢ b`
   (`paperLE_irrefl_pointB`), as does `(false, {true})` over `Bool`. So it is the
   strict part, and the order is its reflexive closure — which is exactly the
   identification §7.4 then performs by hand.
2. The worked example reverses its own definition (`b ⊢ a` where both papers give
   `a ⊢ b`).

A rival repair (Smyth order on covers) agrees at stages 0–2; **the paper's own
element counts select between them** — 1, 2, 5, **20** for the adopted reading
against 1, 2, 5, **21** for Smyth. The adopted reading is the one in `MPair.le`.
If your colimit's stage counts disagree with 1, 2, 5, 20, that is a signal to
stop and re-read, not to adjust the counts.
