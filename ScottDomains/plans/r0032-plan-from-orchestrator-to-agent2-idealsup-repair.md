# r0032 agent2 — Repair `idealSup`, the third instance of one defect

## Goal

Make `IdealCompletion`'s supremum correct on **bounded** families, then reinstate
the bounded-completeness instances it currently makes false — for the Hoare and
Smyth powerdomains, and for any other ideal completion.

You found this defect in r0031; this plan is the repair.

## The defect, precisely

`IdealCompletion.idealSup` branches its `dite` on `Order.IsIdeal (⋃₀ …)`. That is
the membership condition for the **union**, but the union is the least upper bound
only when the family is **directed**. For a merely **bounded** family the least
upper bound is the ideal *generated by* the union, so `sSup` returns `⊥` — not
even an upper bound. Kernel-checked in `P N`: `{0}` and `{1}` are incomparable
compacts, `S = {↓{0}, ↓{1}}` is bounded above by `↓{0,1}`, and `⋃₀ S` is not
directed.

This is the **third** instance of one class: `ScottHom`'s `sSup` (repaired
r0006–r0011), `Smash`'s `smashSup` (repaired r0027 only after the resulting
`BoundedComplete` claim was proved false by the kernel), and now this. Each
branched on a merely *sufficient* condition instead of on the proposition the
constructor actually needs. Both earlier repairs are worth reading before you
start — `ScottHom.lean`'s docstring and `Smash.lean`'s section "What `smashSup`
branches on (r0027)".
