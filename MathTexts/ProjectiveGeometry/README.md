# Projective Geometry — Major Texts, Ranked (for a mathematically sophisticated / logician reader)

Curated for a reader like Dana Scott: foundational and axiomatic treatments are weighted
heavily, alongside the standard modern and algebraic textbooks. For each text the table
records whether a **legitimately free** PDF exists (public domain / open / author-authorized —
no piracy or shadow-library sources) and whether that PDF has an **extractable text layer**
(real OCR/text) versus scanned-image-only or borrow-only.

Availability verified against archive.org item metadata (download formats, OCR full-text,
copyright/lending status) and publisher pages, July 2026. Nothing was downloaded.

## Availability summary

**2 of 10 have a legitimately-available extractible-text PDF** (Veblen & Young; Cremona).
Adding the public-domain bonus classics, the legitimately-available extractable set is:
Veblen & Young (Vols I–II), Cremona, Whitehead, and Filon. All modern textbooks (#2–#9) are
in copyright and available for free only as borrow-only Controlled Digital Lending scans
(no extractable download) or are paywalled.
