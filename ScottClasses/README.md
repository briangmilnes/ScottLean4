# Dana S. Scott — CMU Course Materials (Mathematica Notebooks)

Dana S. Scott (Turing Award 1976; Hillman University Professor of Computer Science,
Philosophy, and Mathematical Logic at Carnegie Mellon University) developed
Mathematica-based teaching materials for his CMU courses. His CMU pages under
`www.cs.cmu.edu/~scott/` and the CS academic class tree are largely gone from the
live web; **all materials below were recovered from the Internet Archive Wayback
Machine** (`web.archive.org`).

## Courses verified

### CS 15-491 A — Computational Projective Geometry (Fall 1998)
Cross-listed across all three of Scott's departments:

| Dept | Number | Title |
|------|--------|-------|
| Computer Science (undergrad) | **15-491 A** | Computational Projective Geometry |
| Computer Science (graduate)  | **15-859 B** | Special Topics in Theory: Computational Projective Geometry |
| Mathematics (undergrad)      | **21-450**   | Topics in Geometry |
| Philosophy (undergrad/grad)  | **80-414 / 80-714** | Computational Projective Geometry |

Plane projective geometry implemented in Mathematica, building toward the projective
classification (over the complex numbers) of cubic curves.
Source root: `www.cs.cmu.edu/afs/cs.cmu.edu/academic/class/15491A-f98/class/`

### CS 15-499 B — Computational Algebra (Fall 2000)
Course page title: "15-499B Computational Algebra". Tu/Th 9:00–10:20, Wean Hall.
Source root: `www.cs.cmu.edu/~scott/Algebra499/`
(Archived early in the term; only the intro notebook and first two topic notebooks
had been posted at capture time.)
